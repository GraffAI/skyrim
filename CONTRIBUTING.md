# Contributing

This repository is a read-only mirror. It is published automatically from the
`projects/skyrim` zone of GraffAI's private `world` tree, where all work on it happens;
every commit here is generated from a commit there.

Issues and pull requests are not accepted. Anything pushed or merged here would
be overwritten by the next publish, and nothing here flows back to `world`.

You are welcome to fork it under the terms of its license.
